# Decision packet

Use for a choice that materially affects cost, scope, risk, schedule, architecture, external commitments, or irreversible action.

## Format

```text
Context: [two lines maximum]

A) [option] — [primary tradeoff]
B) [option] — [primary tradeoff]
C) [optional option] — [primary tradeoff]

Recommendation: [option], because [reason].
Decision needed: [one exact sentence].
Deadline: [date/time or "none"].
No-decision consequence: [what happens].
Choose: A, B, or C.
```

## Rules

- Present two or three materially distinct options.
- Do not use an option merely to pad the list.
- Lead with a strong recommendation when evidence supports one.
- For a reversible decision within authorized scope, state the default and proceed.
- For a consequential decision, stop at the choice boundary.
- Record the selected option, reason, scope, source, and date in state.
